A birthday present for Vlad Bodmodov, made out of Auris's own jewellery
photography.

Merge pieces until you reach thirty-five — one point for each year — and the game
resolves into a catalogue page for a collection named after him.

## Playing

Open `index.html`.

Drag a piece onto an identical piece and the two become one piece a size larger.
Tapping one and then the other works too, which is easier on a phone. Pieces only
combine within their own collection, and the colour of the ring around each token
tells you which one it belongs to. The third size is a finished piece: it swells
to the middle of the tray, names its collection, and goes into the ear.

The ear is the score. Seven piercings, and each finished piece fills one.

**It cannot be lost.** If the tray ever fills up with nothing left to merge, the
oldest piece quietly goes back in the pouch. Nobody loses their own birthday.
